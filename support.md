# ExNoTrk Support

Welcome to ExNoTrk support.

ExNoTrk is designed to bring notes, productivity tracking and personal finance organization together in one application.

## Contact

For help with ExNoTrk, email:

**exnotrk.support@gmail.com**

## When contacting support

Please include:

- Your ExNoTrk app version
- Android version
- A description of the problem
- Steps that reproduce the problem
- Screenshots when useful

Please do not send:

- Passwords
- PINs
- OTPs
- Payment-card numbers
- Banking passwords
- Other unnecessary sensitive information

## Premium support

For Premium-related problems, provide relevant Google Play purchase information only when necessary.

Do not send complete payment-card information.

## Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy for information about how ExNoTrk handles data.

## Data deletion

If you need help deleting your ExNoTrk information, contact us using the email above.
