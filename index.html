How it works

Up and running in minutes

1

Install the app

Download ExNoTrk from the Google Play Store. No account needed to start — open the app and begin immediately.

2

Add your notes and expenses

Create notes, log your first expense, or set a monthly budget. Bank SMS auto-import sets up your expense history in seconds.

3

Split with your group

Create a split group, share the invite code, and start adding shared expenses. ExNoTrk calculates who owes what automatically.

4

Back up when you're ready

Sign in with Google to enable Drive backup. Your data is encrypted before it leaves your device. Restore any time on any phone.

Privacy first

Your data never leaves
your control

📱

On-device storage

All notes and expenses are stored locally in an encrypted database on your phone. Nothing is uploaded without your action.

📧

Gmail read-only

If you grant Gmail access, ExNoTrk reads only bank transaction emails to auto-import expenses. No emails are stored or transmitted.

💬

SMS stays on device

Bank SMS messages are read locally for expense detection. They are never sent to any server — processing happens entirely on your phone.

🗄️

Your Drive, your backup

Cloud backup goes directly into your personal Google Drive. ExNoTrk has no access to any Drive files outside the app's own folder.
