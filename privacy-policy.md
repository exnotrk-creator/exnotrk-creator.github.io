# ExNoTrk Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 24, 2026

ExNoTrk is a personal notes, productivity and finance-organizing application developed by an independent developer.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ExNoTrk may process, why it is processed, and how you can control or delete your information.

## 1. Information stored on your device

ExNoTrk is designed to keep the majority of your personal application data on your Android device.

Depending on the features you use, information stored locally may include:

- Notes and books
- Tasks and checklists
- Goals
- Expenses and income
- Reminders
- Tags and categories
- Transaction information
- App settings and preferences
- Selected themes, colours, fonts and backgrounds
- Security settings
- Other information that you create inside ExNoTrk

## 2. SMS and bank transaction scanning

If you choose to use SMS transaction scanning, Android may request SMS permissions.

ExNoTrk uses SMS access for the transaction-import functionality.

Relevant financial/payment messages may be processed to identify transaction information such as:

- Transaction amount
- Transaction date
- Merchant or description
- Transaction type

The application is designed to identify relevant transaction messages and avoid treating unrelated messages such as OTP or promotional messages as financial transactions.

Imported transaction information is stored in ExNoTrk according to the application's data-storage functionality.

SMS content is not sold or used for advertising.

You can control or revoke Android permissions through your device settings, subject to Android's permission system and the availability of the related feature.

## 3. Gmail transaction import

If ExNoTrk provides and you enable Gmail transaction-import functionality, Google authentication and the permissions you grant are used to access relevant Gmail information required for that feature.

The feature is intended to identify transaction-related information from eligible messages.

You can revoke Google account access through your Google Account settings.

ExNoTrk does not sell Gmail information.

## 4. Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is an optional feature.

If you use this feature, you must sign in with Google and grant the requested permission.

Your ExNoTrk backup may contain information that you choose to back up, including notes, transactions, settings and other application data included in the backup.

Google Drive is operated by Google and is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

You can revoke ExNoTrk's Google access through your Google Account settings.

## 5. Firebase services

ExNoTrk uses Firebase services for certain application functionality.

Depending on the features you use, Firebase may be involved in:

- Authentication
- Shared or split functionality
- Notifications
- Premium entitlement information
- Application backend functionality

Firebase may therefore process technical information and account identifiers required for these functions.

ExNoTrk does not intentionally use Firebase as a general-purpose cloud storage location for your complete local notes and finance database.

Firebase is provided by Google and is subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 6. Notifications

ExNoTrk may use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver application notifications.

You can control notification permissions through Android settings.

## 7. Microphone and voice features

If you use a voice-related feature in ExNoTrk, the application may request microphone permission.

The microphone is accessed only when the relevant feature is being used.

ExNoTrk does not intentionally use the microphone for continuous background recording.

## 8. Smart features and local processing

Some ExNoTrk features use application logic and local processing to organize, calculate, search, categorize, summarize or present information.

Feature names such as "Smart Capture", "Smart Timeline", "Ask your App" or "Budget Pulse" do not mean that ExNoTrk necessarily uses an artificial intelligence model.

ExNoTrk does not intentionally send your personal notes or financial information to an external AI model or AI provider as part of these features.

## 9. Premium subscriptions

ExNoTrk offers optional Premium features through Google Play.

Premium may be offered at:

- ₹99 per month
- ₹999 per year

A 7-day introductory trial may be available to eligible users.

Payments, subscription management, renewals and cancellations are handled by Google Play.

ExNoTrk does not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

Purchase and subscription information may be verified through Google Play developer services so that Premium access can be determined.

## 10. Security

ExNoTrk uses Android security mechanisms and application-level protections intended to protect stored information.

Security-related information such as an application PIN is intended to be protected using secure credential-handling techniques rather than intentionally storing the user's PIN as plain text.

No security system can guarantee absolute security.

You should maintain backups of important information.

## 11. Advertising and analytics

ExNoTrk does not intentionally display third-party advertising.

The application is not intended to sell personal information to advertisers.

## 12. Children's privacy

ExNoTrk is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 13. Data sharing

ExNoTrk does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party services when you explicitly use features that require those services, including services provided by:

- Google Firebase
- Google Play
- Google Drive
- Gmail and applicable Google APIs
- Firebase Cloud Messaging

These services operate under their respective terms and privacy policies.

## 14. Data deletion

Most ExNoTrk information is stored locally on your Android device.

You can delete information using the application's available deletion controls.

Uninstalling the application removes its private application data from the device, subject to Android behavior and any backups that you have separately created.

If you use Google Drive backup, backup data stored with Google must be deleted from Google Drive or through the applicable Google controls.

For assistance with privacy or deletion requests, contact:

**exnotrk.support@gmail.com**

Additional information is available on our Data Deletion page.

The latest version will be published on this page.
