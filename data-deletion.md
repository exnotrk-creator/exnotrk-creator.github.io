# ExNoTrk Data Deletion

ExNoTrk is designed primarily as an on-device application.

You can remove your ExNoTrk information using the methods below.

## 1. Delete local application data

Most ExNoTrk notes, transactions, reminders, goals, tasks and settings are stored locally on your Android device.

You can use the application's available deletion controls to remove individual information.

You can also uninstall ExNoTrk to remove its private application storage from your device.

Before uninstalling, make sure you have created any backup you want to keep.

## 2. Delete Google Drive backups

If you have used Google Drive backup, your ExNoTrk backup files may be stored in Google Drive.

You can delete those backup files using the applicable ExNoTrk controls or through your Google account/Google Drive controls.

## 3. Revoke Google access

If you connected a Google account to ExNoTrk, you can revoke ExNoTrk's access through your Google Account settings.

Revoking access prevents ExNoTrk from using the Google permissions you previously granted.

## 4. Premium information

If you purchased Premium through Google Play, Google maintains applicable purchase and subscription records according to Google's policies.

Uninstalling ExNoTrk does not necessarily delete your Google Play purchase history.

Subscription cancellation should be performed through Google Play.

## 5. Request assistance

If you need help with a deletion or privacy request, contact:

**exnotrk.support@gmail.com**

Please describe the information you want removed.

Do not send:

- Passwords
- PINs
- OTPs
- Payment-card numbers
- Banking passwords
- Other unnecessary sensitive information

## 6. Third-party services

Some information associated with third-party services may need to be removed through those services.

For example, Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google Play.

Google Drive data is controlled through your Google account and Google Drive.

## 7. Processing requests

We will review legitimate deletion requests and take reasonable steps to remove information that we control, subject to applicable legal, security and technical requirements.
