Data Deletion

How to delete your ExNoTrk data — on device and from cloud services

ExNoTrk stores most data locally on your device. Cloud data (split groups, share links, and optional Drive backup) can be deleted at any time using the steps below.

Delete data from within the app

1

Delete local notes and expenses

Open ExNoTrk → Settings → scroll to Data → tap Clear all local data. This permanently removes all notes, books, expenses, and reminders from your device.

2

Revoke share links

Open the note or book → tap the share icon → tap Revoke link. The shared content is immediately deleted from Firebase Firestore.

3

Leave or delete a split group

Open the split group → Settings → Delete group (if you are the creator) or Leave group. Group data is removed from Firebase Realtime Database when the creator deletes the group.

4

Delete your Drive backup

Open ExNoTrk → Settings → Backup → Delete cloud backup. Or open Google Drive → tap the three-dot menu → Storage → find ExNoTrk → delete.

5

Revoke Google account access

Go to myaccount.google.com/permissions → find ExNoTrk → click Remove access. This revokes Gmail and Drive permissions immediately.

Request deletion by email

If you want us to manually verify and delete any server-side data tied to your account, email us with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the Gmail address used to sign in. We will confirm deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted and when

  • Local app data — instantly on clear, or when you uninstall the app
  • Share links — instantly on revoke, or automatically after 30 days
  • Split group data — when creator deletes the group, or auto-archived 90 days after settlement
  • Drive backup — instantly when deleted from app or Google Drive
  • Firebase auth account — within 30 days of deletion request
