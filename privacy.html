Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Applies to ExNoTrk Android app v1.0 and above

Short version: ExNoTrk stores all your data locally on your device. We do not sell, share, or monetise your personal information. Optional cloud features use your own Google account.

1. Who we are

ExNoTrk is an independently developed Android application. The app is operated by a solo developer. You can contact us at exnotrk.support@gmail.com.

2. What data ExNoTrk collects and why

2a. Data stored locally on your device (no server)

The following data is created and stored entirely on your phone in an encrypted local database:

  • Notes, notebooks, tags, colours, and reminders you create
  • Expense records, categories, and budget limits you set
  • Split group names, members, and expense records
  • App preferences and settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an optional cloud feature described below.

2b. SMS messages (optional, on-device only)

If you grant the READ_SMS permission, ExNoTrk reads bank transaction SMS messages on your device to automatically create expense records. This processing happens entirely on your phone. No SMS content is ever transmitted to any server, stored in any cloud, or accessed by the developer.

2c. Gmail (optional, on-device processing)

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, ExNoTrk requests read-only access to scan for bank transaction emails. This access is used solely to extract transaction amounts and merchants so they can be added as expense records in the app. ExNoTrk does not store email content, does not access personal or promotional emails, and does not transmit email data to any server.

Gmail data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the expense auto-import feature within ExNoTrk. It is not used for any other purpose, is not shared with any third party, and is not used to serve advertising.

2d. Google Sign-In (optional)

If you sign in with Google, ExNoTrk receives your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. This information is used to display your account in the app settings and to identify your data for cloud backup and split group features. It is not shared with any third party.

2e. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you enable cloud backup, ExNoTrk stores an encrypted backup of your notes and expenses in the app-specific folder of your own Google Drive account. This folder is only accessible to ExNoTrk — no other app can read it, and neither can the developer. Your backup data is counted against your personal Google Drive storage quota (15 GB free).

2f. Group expense split (optional)

If you use the split expenses feature, group data (member names, expense amounts, and split calculations) is synced via Firebase Realtime Database to allow real-time collaboration with group members. This data is tied to your Google account UID and is only accessible to authenticated members of the same group. It is deleted when a group is settled and archived (after 90 days).

2g. Note and book share links (optional)

If you share a note or notebook via a link, the content of that note or book is stored temporarily in Firebase Firestore so that the recipient can view it. Share links expire automatically after 30 days, or sooner if you revoke them. Each user is limited to 10 active shared notes and 10 active shared books at a time.

3. Data we do not collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers
  • We do not track your location
  • We do not access your contacts
  • We do not read OTP, promotional, or personal SMS messages
  • We do not read any email other than bank transaction emails (when Gmail access is granted)

4. Third-party services used

ExNoTrk uses the following Google services, each subject to Google's own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Authentication — manages your sign-in session
  • Firebase Firestore — stores share link data temporarily
  • Firebase Realtime Database — syncs split group data
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — sends split group notifications
  • Google Drive API — stores encrypted backups in your own Drive
  • Gmail API — reads bank transaction emails for expense import (read-only, on-device processing)

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data retention and deletion

Local data on your device persists until you uninstall the app or use the in-app data deletion option in Settings. Cloud data (split groups, share links, Drive backup) can be deleted at any time from within the app. You can also request complete deletion of any server-side data by visiting our Data Deletion page or emailing exnotrk.support@gmail.com.

6. Children's privacy

ExNoTrk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact us at exnotrk.support@gmail.com.
